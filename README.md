# Anaconda HTN Observatory

A browser prototype of the "Anaconda predator on Fluid HTN" design plan, built with three.js. You can watch it but not play it. Two anacondas share a small island with four pools and its people (four to start with, newcomers after that): a giant female that ambushes from the pools, and a smaller **tree anaconda** that lives in a grove of big trees on Reed Pool's west shore and hunts from branches that reach out over the water's edge, and on the ground. Each has its own HTN brain, and a live panel shows what the selected snake's planner is doing. There are no capybaras: people are the only prey.

**[INVENTED] The whole island is snake territory, and both snakes hunt people actively, hungry or not** (design choices of this build, not anaconda biology, see "Territory: the whole island" below). Every person anywhere on the island is an intruder. The two snakes share all of it (their territories fully overlap); the giant is dominant and drives the tree anaconda off when it comes near, and the tree anaconda stays afraid of it. Apart from a short rest after a meal (a couple of game hours) the snakes hunt people at all hours: they search the island where people are likely to be (camp, terminals, paths, drinking spots, places they sensed people lately), ambush there, stalk, cut people off and track them. A meal is digested in at most one game day (one game day is 360 s). A newcomer (a new person with a new name) lands within about half a game day of each person eaten, so there is always someone to hunt. Weather, the people's own memories and the snakes' body state make every day a little different (see "Days that differ" below). Use 30× to watch the days go by.

Open `index.html` in a browser. It loads three.js r128 from jsDelivr, so it needs an internet connection. All shaders are compiled before the first frame (a short "Building the island…" screen covers the build), and the camera starts framed on the pool snake.

## What you see

- **3D view.** Drag to orbit and scroll to zoom. "Follow snake" keeps the camera on the selected snake's head (and lifts it when that snake is up a tree; the grove's crowns fade so you can see it from above).
- **Snake selector.** Tabs at the top of the panel ("Giant female" / "Tree anaconda") choose which brain the panel, the gizmos and the follow camera show. The dot on each tab is that snake's intent colour.
- **The snake.** One continuous body with a flat belly. The pattern (paired round black dorsal spots, black-ringed orange side ocelli, black blotches on a yellow belly, a fine scale grain and a slight ring-to-ring colour variation) is baked into a texture laid out by arc length along the body, so it does not smear on bends. It moves in travelling S-waves (larger when swimming), creeps almost straight with a ripple of muscle when stalking, and holds its head low when prowling. The head is matte and darker on top, with the eyes sunk into their sockets under a brow ridge. Submerged, only the eyes and nostrils break the surface, with a bright meniscus where the head cuts the water; swimming, only the ridge of the back shows; floating, the whole back lies at the surface; when it *sinks low* (disturbed) the eyes go under too. Before a strike on land the neck draws back into an S (no gape). Rings spread from the snout as it swims (a V-wake) and from the nostrils when it breathes. Parts of the body that would overlap are pushed apart (only a balled-up snake and the constriction coils stack on purpose), and the two snakes never pass through each other.
- **Kills.** A kill is held limp on its side in the jaws, then wrapped in loops with its head and shoulders sticking out. In water the coils first lie at the surface with the prey thrashing among them (splashes, spray, rings, squeeze pulses), then sink as the struggle fades and the prey is held under (bubbles). It is swallowed head-first while the loops unwind into a flat coil; the meal shows as a bulge that moves down the body as it is digested. After a drop from a limb, the coils form on the ground while the rest of the body is pulled down off the limb after the head.
- **The island.** Gently rolling ground with worn paths from camp to the terminals and pools, pools with irregular, organic shorelines (the simulation uses the same outline for every shore and depth query), muddy shores sloping down into the basins, reeds with cattails and lily pads at the margins, sand beaches into the sea, broadleaf trees, birches, spruces, shrubs and rocks. The six grove trees are big, kapok-like trees: plank buttresses at the foot, ridged grey-brown bark with moss low on the shady side, thick limbs with swollen collars, leafy sprays over the limb tips and a high, flat-topped crown. Grass and reeds sway in the wind and are pressed down and pushed aside by both snakes' bodies. Pool water has moving ripples, gets darker and less clear with depth, and reflects the sky at grazing angles, so a submerged snake shows as a dim shape.
- **Day and night, and weather.** A sky dome with a sun that turns orange near the horizon, stars and a moon at night, and fog that thickens at night. Nights are dark: moonlight and the flickering campfire are the main light. Dawn and dusk have a warm, low sun and a brighter fill light. The sky drifts between clear, overcast and rain over hours (shown next to the clock): a drifting cloud layer thickens with the cloud cover, overcast greys the sky and dims the sun, and rain adds falling streaks, rain rings on the water and thicker fog. At night the moon glints on the water only at grazing angles.
- **Prey.** People walk, jog and run (leaning forward) with swinging arms and bending knees, crouch-walk low, kneel to drink at the shore, stroll along a shore, work at the terminals, sleep at camp at night, and look around (more often after they have seen a snake). Newcomers fade in, and a swallowed person fades out.
- **Small movements.** The tongue flicks in bursts of 2–6, often when the snake is investigating or picks up a new scent or tremor, now and then while travelling, and rarely when it is settled. The head turns toward whatever it senses; while looking around it makes short looks and holds them; at rest it lies still with a rare small shift.
- **The tree anaconda.** Smaller (about 60% of the giant's length, ~21 m against ~36 m) and lighter, with a more olive-yellow pattern. It climbs trunks in a loose spiral with its belly against the bark, travels along thick limbs, reaches across the short gap between neighbouring crowns, lies along a limb with its head hanging down over the water's edge where people come to drink, and drops onto people passing beneath. It also comes down to hunt on the ground anywhere on the island.
- **Labels and rings.** A label above each snake's head names the current task, its branch and a short reason (the place, or the target and the sense that found it); on a phone it floats a little higher on a leader line so it does not cover the snake. A thin ring under the head (around the coils while it feeds) shows the intent colour (red hunting, gold feeding, teal digesting, purple fleeing); it is faint while the snake feeds and when the gizmos are off.
- **Gizmos:**
  - Yellow fan: the snake's vision wedge (faint, fading with distance).
  - Red ring: its belief about where the target is. The ring's radius is the position error. Hidden once the target is within strike range.
  - Purple rings: vibrations it felt, drawn where it *thinks* they came from (at most the three newest per animal).
  - While the snake feeds, the vision fan, scent dots and lines are toned down and the belief ring, move goal and ambush ring are hidden.
  - Coloured dots: scent marks (5 m apart, fading over 240 s).
  - Red dashed line: from the head to where the snake believes its target is.
  - Pale outline on the water: where the submerged body lies.
  - Teal ring: the ambush site.
  - Orange line: the current move goal.
  - Territory edge: the whole island is both snakes' territory, so the only outline is a faint red ring just inside the island rim.
- **Brain panel** (the task header stays at the top while you scroll; on a phone the controls become one scrolling row and the view takes 60% of the height):
  - Current task and how long it has run.
  - Branch path and MTR.
  - Each executing condition of the current task, marked pass or fail.
  - Plan queue, including paused partial plans.
  - Drives (hunger, digestion, body heat, fatigue, stress, body condition).
  - Per-prey belief confidence and which sense supplied it.
  - All 29 world-state bytes (the plan's 21, four rival bytes, and `Sky`, `RestPref`, `HuntMood`, `Intruder`). The last one that changed is highlighted.
  - The last 32 replans with their reasons.
  - The domain tree. Valid nodes are bright, the current plan path is orange, and the running task is highlighted.
  - The full decomposition log.
- **Click tools:**
  - **Stomp**: makes a strong ground vibration (both snakes can feel it).
  - **Director: rush here**: the Director picks the best eligible snake (alive, not carrying a kill, not mid-strike; of those, the nearest to the click) and fills its root `Slot DIRECTOR` with a `RushTo` sub-domain. The panel switches to that snake.
  - **Poke**: threatens the snake nearest the click (within 20 m; otherwise the selected one). Its stress goes to "threatened", so its Survive branch takes over.
- **Panel extras:** the prey section starts with a belief row for the *other* snake (distance, sense, its published intent), shows how many people are on the island and when the next newcomer lands, marks sleeping people ("asleep"), and, for the smaller snake, marks people that the feared bigger snake is close to.
- **Scenario:**
  - Choose the pool snake: a giant female (calm, patient, prefers to ambush from the water at drinking spots, but now crosses land after people) or a small male (fast, bold, balls up when threatened, may strike again at close, unwary prey). The tree anaconda is always present. Restart resets both.
  - Jump the clock to dawn, day, dusk or night.
  - "Make hungry" sets the selected snake's hunger to starving.

## What is implemented from the plan

| Plan part | Status here |
| --- | --- |
| Part 2: Fluid HTN semantics | A small JS port. It has Select/Sequence/Action/Slot/PausePlan, conditions checked during decomposition and again on dequeue, and executing conditions checked every tick (failure replans in the same tick). It applies PlanOnly, PlanAndExecute and Permanent effects, rolls back a Sequence on failure, and resumes partial plans. A dirty replan must beat the running plan's MTR, otherwise it is rejected (the panel counts rejections). Plan repair: when the winning plan starts with the task that is already running (after instant bookkeeping steps such as `AlreadyInWater` or a `Choose…` step), the running task carries on under the new plan instead of being torn down and restarted. |
| Part 3: senses and beliefs | Sight (range follows the light, sun height and cloud: about 20 m in full day, 12–17 m at dusk and dawn, 8 m at night; 200° wedge, tree-trunk line of sight, 2 s grace). Vibration (silent below 0.5 m/s; carries 30/55/100 m at walking/jogging/sprinting speed; 15% position error; under half the range and weaker while submerged). Water ripples: prey wading, drinking or swimming in the snake's own pool are felt up to 28 m with 6% error (reported as vibration). Scent marks (5 m spacing, ring of 64, 240 s; smelt from 11 m and weighted more at night). Per-prey beliefs whose confidence decays faster the longer there is no contact. The target is the best confidence × preference, with a 20% margin before switching. All prey are people; a person within 25 m counts a little more, and the smaller snake loses interest in a person the feared bigger one is close to. |
| Part 4: drives | Hunger, digestion, body heat, fatigue, stress, body condition (reserves), a preferred body temperature `prefT` and an appetite. Body heat relaxes toward the air (insolation and the day's temperature), the water (buffered), or shade; basking adds heat in proportion to insolation, more slowly for a bigger body. `prefT` drifts slowly from day to day, and the `Thermal` byte is "cold" / "hot" relative to it. Appetite drifts over days (some weeks a snake eats more readily). Digestion and the return of hunger run faster when the snake is warm. Digestion length scales with the individual prey's mass; hunger only grows once the meal is mostly digested, and returns more slowly when the snake is in good condition. Stress comes from people (graded, see below), Poke, or a feared rival. |
| Part 5: world state | The plan's 21 bytes, four rival bytes, and four added bytes (29), all set by one quantiser with hysteresis. `InTree` is real (enter above 1.2 m, leave below 0.6 m). `TimeOfDay` is perceived, not read off a clock (see "Days that differ"). `Claim` is 1 when the snake is working a quarry and 2 when the feared bigger snake is within ~15 m of it (leave at 19 m). Season, Breeding and Shedding stay at 0. The rival bytes and the added bytes are listed below. |
| Part 6: domain | Director slot, Survive, Feed, DefendTerritory (added, see "Territory"), Hunt, Digest, Breed, Thermoregulate, Ambient, Idle, in the plan's priority order. **[INVENTED]** Hunt needs no hunger: it is valid whenever the snake is not heavy with a meal (`Digesting < 2`, which lasts only a short post-meal rest), not exhausted and not lying up after several fruitless waits. Its tactics are those of an ambush predator: StrikeNow (burst from water, drop from a limb, or a braced land strike), WaitInAmbush (a person coming to the pool it lies in), StalkSeen (Sneak / Creep), AmbushCutOff (get ahead of a walking person on their predicted way and coil there), TrackPerson (follow a located person toward where they are heading), Investigate, FollowTrail, and `SearchForPeople` (choose an area where people are likely, go there, then lie in wait in the water at a drinking spot, on a grove limb, or coiled in cover beside a path, by a terminal or at the camp's edge, or sweep the area slowly). The giant no longer keeps a water leash: it crosses land to people, though its route still follows shorelines and cover where it can. Pursue is only for the small male, or for quarry in the water. Kills are dragged at least ~4 m into the pool (from up to 80 m inland) and held under (Drowning). The full list of branches is under "Two-snake domain tasks" below. |
| Part 7: commitment | Lunge has no executing conditions. Tasks with a minimum dwell (Submerge, HoldAmbush, the rest and bask tasks, constriction and swallowing) cannot be cut short by a replan *or* by a failed non-urgent executing condition until the dwell has passed. Only Stress, a Director order, or the task's own break-out (for the ambush: a strike window or prey arriving) can end it sooner. The ambush lasts at least a quarter of the snake's patience. |
| Part 10: Director | RushTo/CampPoint through the slot. Orders clear themselves when they expire, when they finish, or when the snake sees prey. |
| Part 11: ecosystem | Two snakes at once (pool snake + tree anaconda), each with its own Domain instance, context, body and drives. An ecosystem board (§11.2) holds each snake's published intent. There are no prey claims: size and fear decide (see "Two snakes" below). |

### Territory: the whole island (INVENTED: the user's design)

Real anacondas are not territorial (the plan's §1.4 says so: a home range, not a territory). In this build the whole island is snake territory, by the user's choice, and everything below is labelled INVENTED.

- **One territory, shared.** Both snakes claim the entire island: their territories overlap completely, and there is no border, no home range and no "return home". The only gizmo is a faint ring just inside the island rim. Every person anywhere on the island is an intruder.
- **`Intruder` byte** (none / sensed / located / close, a 5 s hold before it drops): from the snake's belief about its target person: a faint, fading belief is "sensed", a located person (confidence ≥ 0.2) is "located", within ~12 m "close". `TrackPerson` uses it.
- **People are hunted at all times, hungry or not.** After a kill the snake swallows, rests a couple of game hours (`Digesting` = heavy only during that rest; digestion itself takes at most a game day), then hunts again while still digesting (sluggish: 0.6× speed). After 3–4 fruitless waits in a row it lies up for half an hour to an hour and a half (`GiveUpAndRest`), then searches again.
- **Searching the island (`SearchForPeople`).** `ChooseSearchArea` scores candidate places: beside the paths into camp and the camp's edge (best at night, when people sleep there; at night the sweep goes right through the sleeping camp), right by a terminal (by day), along a path, the pools' drinking spots (by day and at dusk and dawn; the giant prefers these, from the water), places where it sensed people over the last hours, and for the tree anaconda a grove limb. The score adds its memory of people sensed nearby, how lately it searched there or waited there in vain (so routes differ and there are no fixed rounds), scares there, distance, and (for the tree anaconda) never anywhere near the giant. Then, after a `PausePlan`, it goes there and lies in wait (`WaterAmbushThere`, `LimbAmbushThere`, `AmbushByPath` › `CoilByPath`, facing the terminal, camp or person it watches) or sweeps the area slowly with stops to scent and look (`SweepArea`). Anything sensed on the way is taken up by the higher Hunt branches (stalk, cut-off, track).
- **Cut-off on the predicted way.** `CutAcross` guesses where a walking person is heading (camp, a terminal or a drinking spot roughly ahead of them) and goes to the nearest point on that line it can reach first (or the destination itself), then coils there (`HoldInPath`). Whether it cuts a person off or tracks them (`TrackPerson`) is rolled per quarry, for variety.
- **The giant leaves the water for people.** Its old ~25 m water leash is lifted; its routes still prefer shorelines and cover, and open ground by day stays costly.
- **People unsettle these snakes less.** Being seen or approached by people raises their stress at about a third of the old rate (more so while hunting or holding a kill); the disturbance responses (freeze, slip into water, ball, flee) remain, and the Poke tool still threatens fully.
- **Size and fear on shared ground.** The bigger snake drives the smaller one off when it comes within ~20 m (`DefendTerritory › DriveOutRival`: `ChaseRival`, then a `ThreatStrike` if it is on the ground within reach, in which case it may rarely hold on and eat it, or `GuardBelow` if it is up a tree; then it leaves it alone for an hour or two). The tree anaconda never challenges the giant: it backs off, climbs, freezes and leaves; it hunts a person only where the giant is not about (~28 m), and up a tree with the giant near it chooses a limb ambush and stays up.

### People: the only prey, shared

Four people to start with (Kaia, Mart, Liis, Rasmus). Each is an individual (build, habits, pool preferences). A person who is eaten is replaced within 0.3–0.5 game day by a newcomer with a new name, landing at the island edge away from the snakes, so there is always someone to hunt. Both snakes hunt the same people. People watch for both snakes: awareness builds from whichever snake is most visible, slowly for a motionless coiled snake, and hardly at all for someone asleep at camp at night. People rarely notice a snake that lies still in a tree.

### Two-snake domain tasks and world-state bytes

- **Survive:** `AbandonKill` (Escape: `UpATree` / `FleeOverland`), `RegurgitateAndWithdraw` (now `WithdrawToWater` / `ClimbToSafety` / `FleeOverland`), `DropIntoWater` (tree anaconda on a limb over the water, threatened by people), `FreezeInCanopy`, `ClimbToSafety` (PlanOnly `InTree=1` then `FreezeInCanopy`), `Ball`, `StrikeBack`, `FleeOverland`, `FleeToWater`, all under `Survive › Escape` (threatened, or a feared rival close and within reach). `Survive › Vigilance` (wary of people) is described under "Days that differ".
- **Feed** is a selector: `EatKill` (ReachWater › Kill › Swallow), `Scavenge` (GoToCarcass › TakeCarcass), `TakeOverKill` (ApproachRivalKill › PausePlan).
- **DefendTerritory:** `DriveOutRival` only (people are handled by Hunt).
- **Hunt** (no hunger condition): `StrikeNow` (needs "the bigger rival is not at this quarry"), `MissRecovery`, `KeepClearOfRival`, `StrikeRival`, `Pursue`, `WaitInAmbush`, `StalkSeen`, `AmbushCutOff`, `TrackPerson`, `Investigate`, `FollowTrail` (land branches also need "safe to come down"), `ChangeTactics`, `GiveUpAndRest`, `GroundAmbush`, `CoilByPath` (carry on lying in wait by a path), `SearchForPeople` (ChooseSearchArea › PausePlan › WaterAmbushThere / LimbAmbushThere / SweepArea / AmbushByPath), `TreeAmbush` (`LieOnLimb`, or `RelocateToLimb`: ChooseAmbushLimb › GetIntoCanopy [AlreadyInTree / ClimbTrunk with PlanOnly `InTree=1`] › LieOnLimb, which travels limb to limb itself), `ChangeTactics` and `GroundAmbush` (tree anaconda), `ShorelineForage`, and `AquaticLieInWait` (giant and male only: `Submerge` at a fresh site, `ShiftAlongShore` by day, `RelocateAmbush` by night).
- **Digest:** `DigestAtTreeBase`, `NightReposition`, then the tree anaconda's perch branches, then the water-side branches (not for the tree anaconda).
- **Thermoregulate:** `BaskOnLimb`, `ShadeInCrown`, then the ground and water branches.
- **Ambient:** `ClimbHome`, `CanopyTravel`, `NightPatrol`, and `DayLieUp` (both snakes rest by `RestPref`).
- **Rival bytes:** `RivalNear` (none / sensed / near <20 m / close <12 m, with distance and confidence hysteresis and a 6 s hold), `RivalIntent` (from the board), `RivalKill` (the rival holds a kill on the ground within 35 m and is sensed), `Carcass` (a known carcass that is free and fresh). A change of `RivalNear` for a feared rival may cut short a committed task's dwell, and "feared rival not close (or not within reach up here)" is an urgent executing condition on the limb tasks.

## Days that differ: weather, places, memory and routines

The aim is that no two days look alike and no pattern is easy to spot, without dice deciding behaviour. Variation comes from the world state the planner sees, and every choice goes through the HTN (branches with conditions, world-state bytes with hysteresis, executing conditions, MTR). No task is started by a clock: across 25 game days no task starts at the same minute of the day more than twice.

**Weather and insolation.** Cloud cover drifts toward a "front" that changes every 5–16 game hours, so spells of clear, overcast and rainy weather last hours. On top of that, passing clouds come and go within the hour (a fast component of the cloud in front of the sun). Each calendar day also has its own temperature offset (a warm or cool spell carried over from the day before). Insolation (sun elevation × cloud in front of the sun) drives body heat, how worthwhile basking is, how fast a meal is digested, and prey routines. The quantised sky is the `Sky` byte (clear / overcast / rain, with hysteresis).

**Time of day as the snake feels it.** `TimeOfDay` is not read off the clock. The quantiser compares how bright it looks (sun height, cloud, rain) with the snake's own thresholds, which drift from day to day (each snake has its own dawn and dusk offsets, carried over from the day before), with hysteresis. A dark overcast morning starts its "day" later; a bright evening ends it later. At dusk the tree anaconda goes out to its limb once it is dark enough for it that evening (its own lag, different each day). How far the snakes (and people) see follows the light too.

**How to rest: the `RestPref` byte.** The quantiser scores four ways of resting relative to the snake's drifting preferred temperature `prefT`: *sun* (needs the sun well up, no rain, and enough sun still to come today; worth more when the snake is cool and the sun strong), *dappled* (mild sun, overcast is fine), *cover* (worth more after disturbance, by day, in rain), and *cool* (when warm, in rain, at night, or after disturbance). A way of resting is taken up only when the snake is comfortably cool enough for it and given up only when it is clearly too warm (no shuttling). Each snake also has a slowly drifting mood per way of resting, and a way of resting it has used for hours loses a little appeal. The current choice is held for half a day to a day unless it becomes impossible, and is only replaced by a clearly better one. A rest under way is not given up for a new preference while the snake is comfortable (`Thermal` ok) and undisturbed, for a quarter to half a day: fewer, longer rests. Digest, Thermoregulate and DayLieUp branch on it:

- Pool snake digesting: `BaskOnBank` / `FloatInShallows` (back at the surface in the sun) / `RestInReedsAtEdge` / `RestSubmergedDeep`.
- Tree anaconda digesting: `DigestOnSunnyLimb` / `DigestAlongLimb` / `DigestInFork` / `DigestInShade`.
- DayLieUp (not hungry, not digesting): `LieUpOnBank` / `LieUpFloating` / `LieUpInReeds` / `RestSubmerged`; the tree anaconda `LieUpOnSunnyLimb` / `LieUpAlongLimb` / `LieUpInFork` / `RestInCanopy`. They end by their own session, not at a fixed hour.
- `Thermoregulate › WarmUp` basks until the snake reaches its preferred temperature, and only when the sun is worth it. When it is cold with no sun and the water is warmer than the air, `WarmInWater` rests submerged instead.
- `Digest › NightReposition`: a light digester that is restless (a day or more at one spot, scared there, or getting peckish) moves at night, along the shore or to another pool (pool snake), or to another crown (tree anaconda).
- A rest task gets to its spot itself (Choose… › `Bask`, not Choose… › GoTo… › `Bask`): when the chosen spot is where the snake already lies, it simply changes posture in place.

**Where to rest, wait and hang: sites with memory.** `ChooseBaskSite`, `ChooseShallows`, `ChooseReedSite`, `ChooseDeepSite`, `ChooseRestSite` (night), `ChooseWaterSite`, `ChooseNearbySpot`, `ChooseAmbushLimb`, `ChooseCrown` and the perch choice score many candidate spots:

- Rest spots are about 14 points around each reachable pool, scored by sun now (tree shade, and which way the bank faces the sun), cover (reeds, tall grass, trees), distance (close by day), how near people walk (more so after it was seen), the rival, scare memory, and how recently it used a spot.
- It may simply stay, or change the way it rests where it lies. For example, it surfaces to float where it was resting submerged, or stays on its limb and lies differently. A rest that is only relabelled keeps the existing coil. A wet rest where the body already lies in the water just sinks or surfaces there; otherwise the settling drift into place is short (at most ~8 m, about 0.5 m/s) and the coil forms at a slow creep.
- Coil size and turns vary with the room there; basking and floating snakes lie in loose loops.
- Ambush spots come from fresh scent at the shore, the drinking trails it has seen in use, plus the pool's prey traffic over the past days (`poolMem`, fading over ~2.5 days), minus spots used recently, spots where it waited in vain or missed, spots where it was scared, and (after being seen) the places people walk. [INVENTED] It relocates by day too (people are hunted at all hours); `SearchForPeople` picks the drinking spot it goes to most of the time (see "Territory").

**Hunting fails.**

- After 3–4 fruitless waits in a row a snake lies up for half an hour to an hour and a half (`Hunt › GiveUpAndRest`, `HuntMood` = fasting), then searches for people again. At night, restless after a wait in vain (or not having prowled for hours), it prowls the shallows with its tongue before settling again (`ShorelineForage`), and a peckish snake patrols the shore (`NightPatrol`: one `TravelShore` task through 2–4 stops, sometimes on to a neighbouring pool).
- There is no open-mouth display before a predatory strike: a submerged snake bursts out, a tree anaconda drops, and on land the snake's `Lunge` starts with the neck drawn back into an S (no gape). If the quarry moves out of view during that brace, there is no strike. Strikes miss more often at alert prey, at the limit of reach, and in the open; a person asleep at camp is easy to take. About half to two thirds of strikes succeed.
- After a miss, `MissRecovery` runs `RecoverFromMiss`: it slides back under or freezes, watches, and marks the place as burned. Then a cool-down of 1–2 game hours follows. Only the small male may strike once more at close, unwary prey; the giant and the tree anaconda never strike twice in a row.
- A stalk is given up when the quarry has clearly noticed the snake, or after a while without closing in. A scent trail that leads nowhere is not taken up again at once.

**Meals differ.**

- Constriction lasts until the prey stops struggling. That depends on its mass and individual strength, how well it is wrapped, the snake's size, and drowning in water. Struggles can flare up again.
- Swallowing takes several times longer than the kill: bouts of jaw-walking with pauses, scaled by prey girth against the snake. A half-swallowed prey keeps its progress if the plan is rebuilt. A threat can still make the snake regurgitate.
- [INVENTED] Hunting does not depend on hunger (the user's design): after a meal the snake rests a couple of game hours (heavy), then hunts people again while it digests (sluggish); digestion takes at most one game day (about 11–21 game hours by the person's build). The hunger drive still runs (it shows in the panel and gates the giant's rare attack on the tree anaconda), but no hunting branch needs it.

**Moving like a snake.**

- Long moves, and every move over land, follow an A* route over a 3 m cost grid. Pool water, the shore margin, reeds, tall grass and tree cover are cheap. Open grass, paths and camp are expensive by day (more so for the giant's long body) and cheaper at night. The giant's grid ends ~22 m from water: it never takes a way that leaves the water's reach.
- Moves inside one pool follow the shallows in an arc. The heading wanders a little, and every gait has a smooth, uneven pace (no fixed speeds).
- On land the snake travels stop-and-go. It halts at the edge of cover before crossing open ground, and in the open by day it halts every 2–5 m (every 3–8 m at night) to look and flick its tongue, for longer when it is uneasy. Swimming, it pauses now and then.
- Going home happens by night (or when far out). Inter-pool travel by day is avoided.
- [INVENTED] The snakes hunt by day and by night. Night and dusk are still the better hours: they see and scent better then, people are asleep at camp, and people see far less in the dark.

**Disturbance, habituation and sensitisation.**

- Each person the snake feels or sees within ~16 m adds stress by distance, approach and speed. This is weighted by how exposed the snake is: an open bank more than reeds, reeds more than afloat, afloat more than submerged, and submerged more than up a tree. Being looked at by a person who has noticed it counts almost double, and a person who spots it and runs off shouting is itself alarming (`seenBy`). A hunting snake reads unaware people as quarry instead.
- It habituates only to people who passed without noticing it. A scare (stress reaching "wary" or "threatened") sensitises it and is remembered at that spot for days, and rest and ambush choices avoid the spot.
- `Survive` has two levels. `Escape` covers threatened, or a feared rival close (the old branches). `Vigilance` covers wary of people, by posture: in reeds or tall grass it freezes (`FreezeInReeds`); on an open bank it slides into the water and sinks (`SlipIntoWater` › `SinkDeeper`); floating at the surface it sinks slowly until only the nostrils show (`SinkSlowly`); submerged it sinks lower (`SinkDeeper`); in a tree it freezes (`FreezeOnLimb`); otherwise `FreezeInCover`. The snake then lingers until it has been calm for a while.

**Prey routines.**

- People sleep at camp most of the night, work at the terminals, and visit pools by day according to their own (slowly drifting) habits. Sometimes they stroll along a shore, and they shelter in rain.
- One sighting is enough: a person who has spotted a snake at a pool avoids that pool (and the way past its shore) for a third of a day to most of a day, and tells the others within shouting distance, who avoid it for a shorter while. Everyone avoids a pool where someone was taken for half a day to a day, and after a kill by the tree anaconda people keep off the grove and the trail past it for as long. A kill site is feared for half a day to a day.
- Each pool has a few drinking spots where people usually come down to the water (Reed Pool has two between the grove trees). The snakes remember which spots they have seen in use.

## Deviations from the plan

- **Hunt condition uses `Digesting < 2`, not `Digesting = 0`, and no hunger** ([INVENTED] the user's design: people are hunted hungry or not). "Heavy" lasts only a post-meal rest of one to two game hours. The plan's §6.5 says Hunt becomes valid again at "sluggish" (1), but §6.2 gives the condition as `= 0`. I followed §6.5. Hunt operators move at 0.6× speed while digesting.
- **Hunt also requires `fatigue < 0.85`, and StrikeNow requires that no miss happened in the last 0.3–0.8 game hours** (the small male may follow up once at close, unwary prey). This is the plan's fatigue "risk/energy budget" made concrete. Without it, a snake that keeps missing would keep striking forever.
- **AmbushCutOff waits 30 s after a failed cut-off.** This stops it from re-planning the same impossible intercept over and over.
- **Prey awareness builds up over time** instead of noticing the snake instantly. It builds slower when the snake is lying still or is in grass, and people see much less at dawn, dusk and night. Prey react to a lunge after about 0.25 s. Without these, prey always escaped. A submerged snake cannot be seen. Some strikes miss on purpose: alert prey, prey at the limit of reach, or prey struck in the open sometimes leap aside (overall about 30–60% of strikes succeed).
- **Prey remember danger.** Places where a snake was seen or struck go on a fear map for 4 minutes (a kill site for half a day to a day), and prey avoid them. People who have seen a snake become vigilant for a few game hours, warn people nearby, and avoid that pool for a third of a day to most of a day after one sighting. When a person is caught, the others run to camp and regroup there (or first away, if the way to camp passes the snake). A newcomer replaces each person eaten within 0.3–0.5 game day, from the island edge on the far side from the snakes.
- **Meals are short (the user's design).** Any meal is digested within one game day, and the snake hunts again after a short post-meal rest; newcomers replace eaten people within 0.3–0.5 game day so the island never empties. The plan's multi-day digestion is not used.
- **Territoriality (the user's design, INVENTED).** The plan treats the home range as undefended (§1.4); here the whole island is both snakes' territory, every person on it is an intruder that is hunted (the `Intruder` byte, `TrackPerson`, `SearchForPeople`), and the bigger snake drives the smaller one off (`DefendTerritory`).
- **The Breed, RefugeDuringShed and DrySeasonCluster branches are in the tree but never run.** Mating is deliberately not implemented, and there is no shed cycle or dry-season phase.
- **Only the tree anaconda climbs.** The small male's `RestInTree` still rests at the base of the trunk. The tree anaconda climbs only the six big grove trees (the other trees are too thin to carry it).
- **The tree anaconda is much bigger than a real arboreal anaconda** (about 21 m), because everything here is scaled to the ~36 m giant. Being smaller, it takes longer to kill and to swallow the same prey than the giant does (roughly 25–90 s and 100–250 s, against 5–25 s and 15–125 s), which also gives a rival time to notice the kill.
- **Two snakes, one Director.** Plan §10 has the Director pick the best eligible snake. Here that means the nearest one that is alive, not carrying a kill, and not mid-strike.
- **No capybaras.** Open question 11's non-player prey were tried and removed at the user's request: people are the only prey.
- **Heavy kills far from water are eaten on the spot.** If the nearest water is more than 80 m away, the snake swallows there instead of dragging.
- **Time is compressed:** one day is 360 game seconds. Speed buttons (1×, 3×, 10×, 30×) multiply that.
- **Added world-state bytes:** `Sky` (clear / overcast / rain), `RestPref` (sun / dappled / cover / cool) and `HuntMood` (keen / fasting) are not in the plan's list of 21. They make weather, the choice of how to rest, and giving up a hunt visible to the planner and in the panel.
- **Added drives:** body condition (reserves), a drifting appetite, and a drifting preferred body temperature (`prefT`) are not in the plan's list of drives. Condition and appetite change only how fast hunger returns; `prefT` sets what `Thermal` and `RestPref` count as warm or cool.
- **`TimeOfDay` is perceived.** The plan quantises the clock; here the byte comes from how bright it looks against each snake's own drifting thresholds, so dawn and dusk come at slightly different times for each snake and each day.
- **[INVENTED] The giant leaves the water for people.** Its earlier water leash (never more than ~20 m from water) is lifted so it can hunt people across the island; its routes still follow shorelines and cover where they can, and open ground by day is costly to it.
- **No prey claims.** The plan's §11.2 board keeps claims; here the board only carries published intent, and the `Claim` byte means "the feared bigger rival is at this quarry" (size and fear decide).
- **Plan repair.** A replan that starts with the task already running keeps that task going (see Part 2 above); Fluid HTN would restart it.
